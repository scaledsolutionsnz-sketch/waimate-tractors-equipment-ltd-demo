What we do

Six things we get asked for most

If it runs on a farm, bring it in. Ring first and describe the fault and we will tell you whether it is a workshop job or something we can talk you through on the phone.

Tractor servicing & repairs

Scheduled servicing, engine diagnostics and mechanical repairs across John Deere, Case IH, New Holland, Massey Ferguson and the rest. We work on what you already run.

Farm equipment repairs

Balers, mowers, spreaders, cultivation gear and harvesters. Tell us what it is doing and we will find where the fault actually sits before we start pulling it apart.

Hydraulic repairs

Cylinder rebuilds, pump and valve work, and hose replacement. A burst hose is usually a same day fix if you can get the machine to us.

Welding & fabrication

MIG, TIG and stick work on frames, drawbars, buckets and yard gear. When the replacement part no longer exists, we make one that fits.

Pre-season checks

Book the gear in before cultivation or harvest starts. We go through fluids, filters, belts and hydraulics, then tell you what needs doing now and what will hold another season.

Parts sourcing

We order genuine and aftermarket parts across the common tractor and implement brands, so you are not spending an afternoon ringing round suppliers.

About us

Ten minutes away, not two days away

Waimate Tractors & Equipment works out of Minnie Street, which puts us inside half an hour of most farms in the district. That gap matters when a machine stops mid job and the alternative is waiting for someone to drive down from Timaru or Christchurch.

We take on all brands and most machinery, from tractors and cultivation gear through to hydraulics and one off fabrication. Some jobs take an hour. Some are a full rebuild over a fortnight. Either way you get told what it needs, roughly what it will cost, and when you can come and get it.

  • All brands, all makes, no dealer network in the way
  • Urgent breakdowns get looked at first during the season
  • You deal with the person doing the work, start to finish
